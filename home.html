About this project

Nahash's Descendants is a digital library dedicated to the analysis of the book of Genesis, bringing together books, articles, maps, and study resources in one place.

How it's organized

The books are the complete texts of the project. Articles are published gradually, based on chapters from the books and on answers already written on Quora. The podcast is made up of audio summaries created with NotebookLM. Maps and resources round out the study material.
